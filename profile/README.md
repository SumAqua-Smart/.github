# 🐟 ŠumAqua Smart

### Smarter Aquariums. Open to Everyone.

**Open-source hardware. Affordable technology. Smarter aquariums.**

Welcome to **ŠumAqua Smart**, an open-source project dedicated to making smart aquarium technology accessible to everyone.

We believe that monitoring and automating an aquarium shouldn't require expensive proprietary equipment, complicated installations, or mandatory subscriptions.

Whether you have a small planted nano aquarium, a large community tank, or an entire fish room, our goal is to give you the freedom to build a smarter aquarium your way.

---

## 🌱 Our Philosophy

ŠumAqua Smart is built around five principles:

- **Affordable by design** — Use accessible, commonly available electronic components wherever practical.
- **Open-source and transparent** — Share firmware, software, hardware designs, and documentation.
- **Modular and expandable** — Start with one device and expand as your aquarium grows.
- **Local-first** — Essential device functions should continue working without an internet connection.
- **Freedom of choice** — Use devices independently, connect them to a self-hosted controller, or choose optional managed services.

**Your aquarium. Your hardware. Your data.**

## 🔧 What We're Building

ŠumAqua Smart is an ecosystem of Wi-Fi-connected aquarium devices powered primarily by ESP32 microcontrollers.

### 💡 Smart Light

Affordable aquarium lighting with adjustable brightness, customizable schedules, and sunrise/sunset transitions.

### 🌡️ AquaProbe

Modular aquarium monitoring, beginning with water temperature and potentially expanding to pH, water level, and other parameters.

### 💧 Smart Water Management

Future tools for water changes, top-offs, and monitoring, designed with safety and reliability in mind.

### 🐠 Fish Room Monitoring

A longer-term goal to monitor and manage multiple aquariums, room conditions, and connected devices from one interface.

## 🖥️ Software Ecosystem

We're designing ŠumAqua Smart around three complementary components.

**Device Firmware**

ESP32-based firmware that allows devices to operate independently, communicate over Wi-Fi, and retain essential settings locally.

**ŠumAqua App**

A planned unified interface for device setup, monitoring, lighting control, and aquarium management.

**ŠumAqua Controller**

An optional self-hosted controller for historical data, dashboards, notifications, and advanced automation.

Our longer-term vision also includes optional hosted services for aquarists who prefer not to maintain their own servers.

## 🚧 Project Status

**Early development — hardware prototyping**

We're starting small, with two initial prototypes:

- **Smart Light V0.1:** ESP32-C3-controlled white LED aquarium lighting.
- **AquaProbe V0.1:** ESP32-C3-based water temperature monitoring using a DS18B20 sensor.

The project is currently experimental. Hardware designs, firmware, software, and documentation will evolve as development progresses.

No production-ready devices are available yet.
